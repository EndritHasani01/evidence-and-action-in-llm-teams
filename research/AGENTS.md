# Working with completed research

- Start with `../PROJECT_SPECIFICATION.md`, the final authority, then `README.md` and the relevant entries in `INDEX.md`.
- Treat every phase here as historical research or supporting evidence. Earlier authority labels and prospective instructions apply to their original phase. Do not combine superseded plans into implementation requirements.
- Use `manifest.json` to iterate files and translate original repository paths into current locations. Read only relevant phases rather than ingesting the whole archive.
- Distinguish executed CPU checks from proposed experiments, unmeasured forecasts and unexecuted inference. A historical check does not validate the final experiment.
- Preserve stored snapshot metadata when citing historical evidence. Rerunning a probe overwrites its adjacent result file; explain when a report is newly regenerated.
- Keep `acl26-silo-bench/` at the repository root as the vendored source/data checkout.
