# Novelty attack: historical before-values in a correction receipt

Investigated **4 October 2026** after the main investigation identified this gap in the PS. This is an independent adversarial review, not an efficacy finding.

**Proposal-history status:** the AUC interaction discussed below was the initial candidate. The final experimental proposal in [FINALIST_EXPERIMENTS.md](../FINALIST_EXPERIMENTS.md) and [NOVELTY_ADVERSARY.md](../NOVELTY_ADVERSARY.md) uses the first-response interaction `eta_1` as the focused estimand on predetermined prefix/sort worlds, with `eta_A` a mandatory descriptive AUC companion. The original six-family `theta` stays primary. This source note does not supersede that refinement or the unchanged governing PS.

## Candidate and immediate verdict

The PS removes obsolete raw records from active source tables; retain-history B may still contain their old values in prose, while reset C removes those histories. Both have identical **current** source evidence, but they need not have identical **historical transition** information. A receipt carrying authenticated `record, old_version, historical_old_value, new_version, current_new_value` would give B and C the same explicit transition observation without exposing gold answers or semantic footprints.

This is a scientifically coherent additional factor. It preserves the original six-task study and isolates an interface decision a real runtime can make. **It is not a novel mechanism or a newly discovered need for before-images.** Its possible originality is a causal boundary result: conclusions about retaining reasoning may depend on what the correction API tells the team.

The original C−B estimand remains valid. Historical information lost with history is part of that total intervention; calling it a fatal confound would misread the PS. The extension refines interpretation rather than repairing an invalid experiment.

## Closest agent threats

### StateAuditor's transition assembly

[When Memory Updates but Behavior Does Not](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.01619), §§4.3–4.4 and §6, is the closest inspected predecessor. It extracts previous/current states, evidence quotations and chronology; its transition proposal feeds typed behavioral repair. A same-evidence/call-budget control removes transition machinery. The paper also compares timeline-conditioning and auditing, and reports boundaries where transitions add little.

Therefore “show old and new state to make agents revise” is already occupied. The proposed experiment differs by using an exact runtime-owned edit rather than inferred semantic transitions, factoring receipt content against retention of a genuinely shared team history, and evaluating exact affected/unaffected outputs. These differences make a narrower empirical question; they do not establish firstness or a superior general algorithm.

### Update wording and compliance

[What It Costs to Compose, Rebuild, and Correct Precomputed Memory](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.30647), §6 and the wording-factorial appendix, tests bare updates, wrapped updates, question naming, labels and combined anchors. Its false-value control shows that strong uptake can reflect compliance rather than verification.

Consequently, BΔ/B or CΔ/C differences are effects of a **receipt package** unless the experimental controls support a narrower reading. The old value changes length, lexical overlap, salience and available information simultaneously. A consistent structured layout with a missing-before marker versus the true historical before-value is a reasonable minimal control; extra neutral padding may reduce token-count differences but cannot establish identical attention. Do not call this arithmetic delta understanding solely from improved final answers.

### Explicit revision benchmarks

[DeltaLogic](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.02733), protocol appendix, exposes an original premise set, then its revised set, and measures inertia/over-flipping. It does not factor evidence-only reset against before/after receipts. [RIAG](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.01249), §§3–4, separates current and historical task bindings but keeps task payloads fixed. These defeat broad claims about novel revision or temporal-role evaluation, while leaving the specific distributed retention interaction unestablished by the inspected experiments.

The prior workspace's [execution-state unlearning](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.04875v1) reference asks for a never-observed counterfactual. The receipt candidate deliberately pursues a weaker operational objective: make correct use of a past value *as past*. It must not claim erasure or noninterference.

## Reductions to known systems

1. **Change-data capture:** the record is an ordinary before/after event. No algorithmic novelty remains at the data-format level.
2. **Incremental computation:** if the old output is a valid sufficient statistic and the operator has known delta semantics, a deterministic updater can exploit the receipt. That is an explanatory diagnostic, not an LLM discovery or a runtime gold solver.
3. **Temporal databases/event sourcing:** old truth remains valid for its historical interval. Separating historical evidence from current authority is classical.
4. **Prompt engineering:** if a same-information rewording produces the same effect, the useful finding is an interface sensitivity, not a new information-theoretic limit.
5. **Reclaim-style source retention:** a receipt supplies a small missing piece of source history; generic source-versus-conclusion retention already has a close predecessor. The new contrast must specifically concern the **transition × reasoning-retention interaction**.

## Why the factorial can still teach something

Define, within the same changed world and prefix,

`eta = (A[C_delta] - A[B_delta]) - (A[C] - A[B])`.

This asks whether the reset contrast changes when both treatments receive an explicit old-to-new receipt. It does not identify a psychological cause. The main PS interaction with sham remains separately valuable and should remain primary unless a later specification review explicitly changes it.

Four possible outcomes are informative:

- **Receipt benefits B more:** preserved aggregates or decisions become easier to update when the transition is explicit. Recovery depends jointly on useful old work and change information.
- **Receipt benefits C more:** clean recomputation may exploit the transition, or the receipt may focus attention; an additional interpretation check is required before attributing delta reasoning.
- **Receipt helps both equally:** correction rendering matters, but the original retention conclusion is stable.
- **Receipt harms both or increases stale values:** retaining historical evidence may confuse temporal roles under this model/protocol. The historical label's adequacy is an empirical question.

These are predictions, not expected results. A precisely bounded null AUC interaction establishes stability of the AUC retention contrast, not that the receipt has no value: equal large benefits in both arms also give zero interaction. Preferring the simpler correction interface additionally requires sufficiently bounded direct benefits, harms and costs. The final first-response refinement has the same caution and does not imply greater power merely because its raw contrast is larger.

## Important counterexamples and identification limits

- With all current sources available and unbounded exact computation, the old value is unnecessary to calculate the current answer. The candidate concerns bounded computation and retained intermediate work, not a universal information deficit.
- CΔ has no old candidate/aggregate. A before-value alone does not magically enable incremental updating of a deleted sufficient statistic. This makes an opposite-to-intuition interaction entirely plausible.
- In some tasks or histories B already exposes the exact old value. Then BΔ adds redundancy and an authoritative temporal label, while CΔ adds information. Measure this occurrence from prompts; do not silently infer it from source delivery.
- A raw before-value may reveal historical information to peers that never held that record. This is permitted if it is the intentional correction-delivery factor, identical for both new branches, and charged in communication/context accounting.
- A tombstoned BFS edge must have identifiable endpoints under the frozen schema. If record identity already reveals the old edge, the receipt adds no new factual information there; classify that case honestly rather than pooling it as equivalent to hidden numeric before-values.
- Text-free exact delta or semantic affected-coordinate labels would change the intervention. Send only raw old/current records and version roles; keep the gold footprint offline.
- More token budget or stronger instructions in delta branches would invalidate the intended receipt comparison. Preserve call/output budgets and use the same generic correction instruction.
- A pilot can assess protocol/capability and budget, but cannot choose the receipt design based on the desired eta sign.

## Candidate-only arm versus ICR

Retaining only a final candidate while clearing rationale supplies a meaningful middle state, but [ICR](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.01042v1) already compares answer-only and full-reasoning messages against no-message revision. This adaptation gains its distinction from an actual world change after feedback-rich private-information collaboration, not from a new content abstraction.

A candidate-only arm is also hard to interpret on poor prefixes: it retains wrong old outputs and may discard useful non-output summaries. The receipt factorial more directly targets an unused protocol choice while keeping B/C intact. Candidate-only is an optional later diagnostic, not an automatic extra requirement.

## Comparative judgment

On current evidence, this **focused experimental dimension** has a better fit to the PS and lower implementation burden than replacing the thesis with completion reopening. It has a more precise causal shape than merely correlating exposure-cone size with recovery. Its novelty is defensible only at that limited empirical level, and depends on a result that clarifies when historical transition information changes the value of retained work. If every observed effect reduces to a generic clearer-correction benefit or intervals are uninformative, retain it as a small diagnostic rather than promoting it to the main thesis contribution.
