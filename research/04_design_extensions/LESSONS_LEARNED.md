# Lessons learned

## Initial guardrails

- A novel label does not establish a novel principle; compare observables, assumptions, guarantees, and experimental questions.
- A runtime signal is different from an evaluator's counterfactual truth. Keep the boundary explicit.
- Matched calls do not imply matched token cost, useful computation, or information.
- The existing experiment is specified, not implemented or empirically validated. Preserve that distinction.

## Durable findings

- Losing current authority does not erase an input's historical role. Before-images are established systems practice.
- Equal current source tables need not contain equal transition information. The original total history-removal effect remains valid.
- Update sufficiency depends on representation, visible evidence and edit payload together, not just family or output footprint.
- Difference outputs plus an unchanged anchor reconstruct old values; a local prefix segment may not reveal a remote operand. Actual source/output placement defeats superficial theory.
- A globally sufficient sorted multiset may be unavailable locally. Removed-value identity and remote rank-boundary acquisition are separate problems.
- Complete transitions cannot recreate alternatives discarded from max/top-K/BFS outputs.
- Correct-summary proofs do not certify model summaries. Efficient updating can preserve an old error.
- Constructed ambiguity establishes possibility, not natural frequency or four-round impossibility. Scripted histories are not model outcomes.
- First recovery round isolates a pre-new-message boundary; conceptual endpoint alignment does not guarantee more power.
- A negative interaction can reflect harm to another arm; a zero interaction can hide large equal benefits. Report direct effects and all cells.
- Early correctness does not establish mechanism or sustained quality. Fixed calls demonstrate no allocated inference saving.
- Temporal order matters beyond static topology. Delivered-content paths omit possible absence, routing and source-selection influence.
- Exposed calls and changed coordinates have different units; no ratio certifies unnecessary invalidation. Correct values do not certify associated rationales.
- CIR/ICR narrow metric/router/content claims; StateAuditor narrows transition repair; TRACE/Commitment/GitHarness/PoS narrow completion proposals. Prior recommendations have no incumbency.
- Preserve known-method reductions instead of hiding them behind new names.
- Extra paired contrasts do not replace independent worlds for free. Protect the original complete sample.
- A precise boundary, measured interaction or justified simpler-policy conclusion can add research substance without a new framework.
