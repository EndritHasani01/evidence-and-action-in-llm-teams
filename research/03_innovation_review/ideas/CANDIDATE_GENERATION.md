# Candidate generation before selection

2026-09-30. These are alternatives to challenge, not promised innovations.

| Direction | Core decision | Potential value | Immediate novelty/failure objection |
|---|---|---|---|
| Retention experiment | Clear derived context or retain it after the same update | Strong causal empirical boundary study | Mechanism contribution absent; still a legitimate fallback. |
| Current-world artifact admission | Quarantine old derived state; reintroduce artifacts through explicit checks | Reuse may not require reconstructing every historical dependency | Certifying algorithms, revalidation, PlanFence and REVISE already occupy much of this space. |
| Change-resilient representation | Store parameterized procedures or obligations rather than fixed answers | A fact edit updates bindings while preserving expensive strategy construction | Incremental computation, cached plans and partial evaluation are established; may turn SILO into ordinary programming. |
| Recovery compartments | Limit cross-agent influence through narrow typed interfaces before any change | Bound conservative invalidation despite opaque internals | Hermetic build rules, fault domains and information-flow control are established; preventive overhead may dominate. |
| Budgeted challenge/inspection | Spend recovery effort checking high-value uncertain reuse first | Useful when checking everything costs as much as restarting | Value of information and cache policy are old; calibrated failure probability may be unavailable. |
| Distributed revision agreement | Agents acknowledge which revision governs outputs; reject late old work | Stops resurrection through asynchronous channels | Epochs, fencing, snapshots and optimistic validation are old; useful infrastructure, weak standalone novelty. |
| Reopen suppressed alternatives | Track decisions that excluded options and revisit them after change | Repairs missing search paths that positive provenance never records | Belief revision, ATMS, nonmonotone queries and plan repair already anticipate negative dependencies. |
| Bounded counterfactual replay | Restore clean contexts and replay only the affected exposure suffix | Strong behavioral cleanup without semantic interpretation | Execution-state unlearning already directly addresses this. |

Selection should favor a tractable empirical question around an explicit runtime capability, not a new label for a row in this table. Seek a failure regime where two credible established methods make different predictions.
