## Evidence exclusions

Search snippets, community posts and surveys were used as leads, not as technical evidence for the final recommendation. No performance claim is based on memory of a prior run. No benchmark/model inference was executed. Unavailable full text is explicitly marked and does not support a negative novelty claim.

No paid service was used, no external account was modified, and no source or specification implementation was changed. Local research scripts and notes are the deliverables of this phase.
