## Classical foundations to cite and use correctly

- [Doyle, A Truth Maintenance System (1979)](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/0004370279900080) and [de Kleer, An Assumption-Based TMS (1986)](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/0004370286900809): retaining conclusions with surviving justifications is old. The proposed antichain of minimal supports is a bounded implementation of that idea, not a new logic.
- [W3C PROV-DM](https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-dm/): entity/activity/agent lineage is not equivalent to logical entailment or a model's internal causal explanation.
- [Chandy and Lamport, Distributed Snapshots](https://lamport.azurewebsites.net/pubs/chandy.pdf): a consistent checkpoint must include communication state, not merely agent prompts. Our centrally scheduled simulator makes a quiescent cut straightforward; we do not solve asynchronous snapshotting.
- [Alchourrón, Gärdenfors and Makinson, On the Logic of Theory Change](https://doi.org/10.2307/2274239): belief revision is not generally identical to deleting records. This experiment adopts finite task semantics rather than claiming a general AGM revision operator.
- [A Study of Belief Revision Postulates in Multi-Agent Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.02249): even the formal extension of revision to multi-agent epistemic models is active prior work. We do not claim the first multi-agent belief-revision formulation.
- [Green, Karvounarakis and Tannen, Provenance Semirings (2007)](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/1265530.1265535): combinations of joint and alternative derivations are classical provenance objects. The support representation here is intentionally bounded and does not introduce a new provenance algebra.
- [Cohen and Levesque, Intention Is Choice with Commitment (1990)](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/0004370290900555): relationships among beliefs, goals, intentions and action are established MAS concerns. Keeping a goal while reconsidering its concrete means is not conceptually new. Publisher metadata was located; these two classic publisher full-text endpoints returned access errors in this session.

The LLM-specific issue is empirical: explicit handoffs and citations may omit the distinctions a classical maintenance algorithm needs, and recipients may fail to implement a correct revision instruction. Opaque cognition is not made transparent by recording text.

## Corpus synthesis used

The main conceptual inputs came from the provided files on shared memory/provenance, failure attribution/recovery, robustness, verification, distributed coordination, organizations, classical MAS, and evaluation science. They motivate: preserving independent support; separating truth, permission, and current validity; testing stale action state after correction; and measuring mechanism-specific outcomes. The enterprise, finance, software, and scientific-team investigations inform structural relevance. Their application domains are not treated as evidence that the mechanism has been validated there.

## Searches and verification limits

Searches covered source correction, dependency-guided rollback, memory cascade repair, incomplete provenance, identified repair sets, action bindings, independent support, multi-agent belief revision, current benchmark code, and classical truth maintenance. Repository checks included exact branches/commits and concrete schema/engine/grader files. A repository-name search with no match is recorded as “not verified,” never “no code exists.”

No full-paper reproduction was run. No author was contacted. Direct access failures for REVISE, MemTX's paper, and the anonymous rollback-family submission are explicit limits. The MemTX code was readable and is separately evidenced above. These limits do not prevent engineering the experiment, but they do prevent an unconditional first-of-its-kind claim.

## Decision rule if the missing full text occupies the narrowed gap

Keep the infrastructure only if it supports a substantive replication or boundary-condition study: natural versus declared support, claim versus action-level support, and downstream-state reconstruction. If the paper already reports all three under paired multi-agent continuations and exact support labels, do not rename the method. Reframe as a preregistered replication on independent infrastructure only if the supervisor accepts that contribution; otherwise abandon this thesis formulation before expensive experiments.

## Claims explicitly prohibited

“First dependency-aware recovery”; “first alternative-support preservation”; “first multi-agent belief revision”; “first transactional agent memory”; “first partial replay”; “causal graph of the model's reasoning”; “general safe recovery guarantee”; “ground-truth free recovery” when an oracle was supplied; “SILO-Bench performance” for modified tasks; “real-world finance safety” from a synthetic transfer.
