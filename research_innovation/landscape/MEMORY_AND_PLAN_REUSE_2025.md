# 2025 foundations: memory updating and procedural reuse

Inspected 2026-09-30. These sources establish that evolving memory and reusable plans predate the proposed recovery study.

| Primary source | Inspection | Relevant boundary |
|---|---|---|
| [LongMemEval](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.10813v2), ICLR 2025, preprint first posted 2024 | Full HTML located; benchmark description and ability categories inspected; [author publication record](https://xiaowu0162.github.io/publications/13_longmemeval/) verifies venue | Explicit knowledge updates, temporal reasoning and abstention. Answering questions about a revised history does not by itself establish recovery of a team's partially completed search. |
| [Zep](https://arxiv.org/html/2501.13956v1), January 2025 | Temporal extraction/edge invalidation method and evaluation scope inspected | Distinguishes event validity from ingestion time and updates contradictory facts. Fresh retrievable facts alone do not certify current plans, search coverage or action validity. |
| [Agentic Plan Caching](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/9549f7d06700f0966d5f938f1d11022a-Abstract-Conference.html), NeurIPS 2025 | Official proceedings abstract only | Extracts, adapts and reuses plan templates across related requests. Parameterized plan reuse cannot be claimed as a new principle here; no detailed recovery comparison is inferred from the abstract. |

The investigation is centered on the newest direct competitors, but should not erase these older distinctions: retrieval, fact revision, procedural reuse and recovery of an already-running collaboration are related yet different evaluation targets.
