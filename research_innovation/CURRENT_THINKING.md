# Current thinking

## Current state — 2026-09-30, second research checkpoint

**Leading direction:** study recovery of search/completion decisions, separately from validity of retained positive work. A revision can make previously excluded or unexamined options important while every old fact and feasible plan remains true. Candidate mechanism: expire the team's permission to treat a question as settled, reopen the relevant search obligation, and reuse surviving evidence/artifacts. This is a translation of established nonmonotone query maintenance, dynamic constraint repair and goal reconsideration; novelty must lie in the practical opaque-agent interface and causal evidence, not a claim to invent negative dependencies.

**Strong alternative:** check old artifacts against current contracts before regenerating them. This is feasible but substantially occupied by certifying algorithms, MemoRepair, REVISE, proof-carrying agents and clean-context regeneration. In SILO a strong checker can effectively solve the task, creating a major internal-validity problem.

**Evidence changing the direction:** REVISE automatically instruments data/control reads and collection membership; it is stronger than the specification's brief description. MemoRepair already studies missing edges and withdraw/repair/validate/republication. ESC-CR reconstructs clean contexts without complete language-level provenance. Reclaim Evaluation directly studies source-preserving versus conclusion-preserving memory. Therefore a new reset/quarantine/graph wrapper is not a strong invention.

**Unresolved:** can useful reopening scopes be generated or maintained without a semantic oracle? Does a coarse 'reopen all completion claims' baseline already suffice? Does a fresh planner with the same evidence and budget do equally well? Are there actual omitted-work failures in natural agent prefixes rather than only authored fixtures? Full control-aware REVISE may subsume the proposed protocol in a structured workflow; this is a possible result, not an objection to hide.

## Initial hypothesis retained for history

The current specification proposes a controlled state-retention experiment rather than a new recovery algorithm. Its useful foundations are matched pre-change checkpoints, source/derived-state separation, no-change controls, exact task outcomes, and a bounded compute budget. These are starting constraints to examine, not conclusions to defend.

The practical question is how expensive distributed work can survive an authoritative change without obsolete work retaining unjustified influence. Recorded dependencies are potentially incomplete; simply deleting known descendants may therefore provide false assurance. Conversely, resetting all reasoning destroys accumulated work and may discard valid alternative routes.

No candidate is selected. Investigation streams: (1) fresh repository inspection and feasibility; (2) verification and adversarial reading of recent agent recovery/memory work; (3) adjacent systems ideas; (4) first-principles candidate generation and causal study design.

Next: establish source trust, enumerate actual runtime state channels, identify what prior work already solves, and compare genuinely different candidate mechanisms before recommending one.
