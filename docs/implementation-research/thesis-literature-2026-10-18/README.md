# Scientific foundations for the thesis design decision

**18 October 2026 · independent literature investigation · recommendations only**

The strongest next study is a small, controlled investigation of **when useful
historical evidence supports team recovery and when an explicit historical
commitment interferes with it**. It should also test whether teams communicate
new private information when that information is necessary. The current evidence
does not justify a large recovery-policy matrix, a sophisticated memory system,
or a presumption that clearing history fixes the observed failures.

This package challenges the [18 October proposal](../../proposals/thesis-extension-2026-10-18.md).
It does **not** adopt, implement, or amend it. The
[specification](../../../PROJECT_SPECIFICATION.md) remains the sole scientific
authority. Every pre-existing repository file, configuration, implementation and
experimental artifact was preserved. No inference, GPU work or new experiment
was performed.

## What should change in our thinking

**1. Repair the task's information requirement before increasing its difficulty.**
In the proposed withdrawal population, the old winner is always replaced by a
value below the remaining maximum. With complete historical shard reports, the
correct answer can therefore be obtained by removing that row from the old
table. This is a valid restricted task, but success need not demonstrate use of
the new value. Add a balanced stratum in which the updated record remains the
winner with a different value. Keep that stratum hidden from peers. This is an
original deduction from our generator and views, informed by research that
exposes task-solving shortcuts through carefully chosen counterexamples; it is
not a finding that our models already exploit the shortcut.

**2. Treat supplied histories as an experimental intervention.**
Complete, correct historical reports make the changed owner capable of computing
the new global result immediately. The remaining team problem is to convey it
and complete both private submissions. This is useful isolation, provided the
claim is conditional on supplied evidence. A separate natural-history bridge
should measure how far that starting state is from histories agents produce.
It cannot turn scripted-history results into end-to-end deployment evidence.

**3. Choose a practical primary contrast and retain the interaction as context.**
Prefer the direct revised-world difference between source evidence alone and
the identical evidence plus an old commitment. Report both no-change cell means
and the revision-minus-no-change interaction. An interaction alone can favor
removing the commitment because the commitment helps only in no-change worlds,
even when removing it never improves recovery. This changes the proposed
estimand, so it requires explicit adoption.

**4. Spend the main budget on independent worlds; use a fixed repeat panel for
repeatability.** The statistical literature distinguishes generalization over
worlds from stochastic reliability on fixed worlds. Two trials everywhere are
not automatically the best allocation. The preferred planning alternative is
192 worlds with one primary continuation per cell, plus two extra trials on 24
predetermined worlds. The extra trials have a separate descriptive purpose.
Neither this allocation nor its precision is established by course data.

**5. Diagnose the path from evidence to action.** Preserve joint exact success
as the outcome. Separately describe whether the needed information was visible,
whether a report associated it with the right owner and record, whether a
correct value was expressed, and whether both agents actually submitted it.
Prose is evidence of an utterance, not proof of an internal belief or an action.
Do not add a host finalizer, numerical validator, or compulsory consensus loop.

## Evidence strength and its limits

| Judgment | Support | Confidence and implication |
|---|---|---|
| Access to information does not guarantee its use or a completed action | Course traces; controlled coordination, memory and explanation studies | Strong motivation for separate observable diagnostics |
| More discussion, dissent, agents or memory machinery need not help | Conflicting debate results and task-dependent memory results | Strong reason to avoid expanding the main condition matrix |
| Source evidence and a historical conclusion are different experimental objects | Information-theoretic reasoning and controlled memory studies | Strong conceptual basis; our models' effect size remains unknown |
| A short common coordination scaffold may improve competence | Positive structured-protocol studies, with important intervention confounds | Plausible development option; no guaranteed transfer to this interface |
| One repeat allocation is optimal for this study | Variance decomposition, but no relevant variance estimates yet | Conditional recommendation, not an empirical fact |
| Qwen and Granite can execute the proposed task reliably within budget | Model documents and short Qwen course experience | Unresolved until separately authorized hardware and capability gates |

The thematic documents explain the supporting studies and their transfer
conditions. Recent preprints are treated as provisional. No external paper
establishes an effective recovery policy for these exact two model systems.

## Recommendation for the next session

Approve a **design decision**, before implementation: a four-cell controlled
comparison of source evidence versus source evidence plus old commitment,
crossed with revision versus no change, using two balanced revision strata and
two separately qualified model systems. Retain small natural-history and real
answer-preserving checks. Move the empty-history arm out of the main factorial.

The [decision document](prioritized-design-tests.md) gives the full candidate,
its alternatives, arithmetic and gates. Its conservative planning ceiling is
**17,000 new attempts**, with a **proposed, unapproved 200 aggregate GPU-hour
ceiling**. At 30 allocated seconds per attempt, its conservative estimate exceeds
that ceiling. The existing 100-hour scientific contract is not silently enlarged.

Approval of this research synthesis alone authorizes no implementation or
execution. The next decision must explicitly accept or reject its amendments,
then separately authorize CPU implementation. GPU development, frozen changes,
main inference, paid compute and publication remain distinct boundaries.
