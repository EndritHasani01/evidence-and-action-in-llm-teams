# I-01 course-7: literature-informed experimental improvement recommendations

16 October 2026. **Independent recommendation; not an adopted amendment.**
This follows the [targeted fifteen-paper review](../implementation-research/i01-course-7-targeted-scientific-literature-review.md),
the [independent scientific audit](../validation/i01-course-7-independent-scientific-audit.md)
and the [earlier improvement proposal](i01-course-7-experimental-quality-improvement.md).
The [scientific specification §12](../../PROJECT_SPECIFICATION.md#12-adopted-i-01-course-regime)
continues to govern the executed study. No implementation, prompt/configuration
change, inference, GPU use, contract amendment or new experimental execution
occurs in this work. Course-7 remains intact.

## 1. Recommendation and decision to make

Use the next bounded research stage to establish **whether historical conclusion
exposure changes adaptation when historical source evidence is held fixed**.
Compare actual source withdrawal with a matched no-change continuation. First
perform a small common-interface competence check; then, if interpretable,
conduct a separately identified controlled-history feasibility study.

This revises the earlier proposal's default emphasis. Prompt clarification is
still a sensible first behavioral candidate, but another natural retain/clear
study should not automatically follow it. Even competent natural agents would
leave useful evidence, old conclusions and history presentation entangled in
that comparison. The controlled question offers greater scientific resolution
per attempted continuation, while making its supplied competence explicit.

If the intended thesis question must remain the **practical total effect of
retaining versus clearing naturally acquired conversations**, choose that target
instead. It is coherent and closer to the adopted contract. It should retain
all worlds and failed prefixes, and make narrower claims. The two targets should
not be combined under one primary estimand.

The recommended next study is a **feasibility and mechanism study**, not a
publication-sized test of a modest policy effect. No intervention is selected
because it is expected to favor retain or clear. A stable null in a capable,
well-controlled setting is an acceptable outcome.

## 2. What the literature changes

Paper IDs refer to the [review's verified bibliography](../implementation-research/i01-course-7-targeted-scientific-literature-review.md#7-verified-bibliography-and-full-text-reading-inventory).

These lessons are not all new to the historical research archive. What is new
at this stage is their relevance to the observed Course-7 system and the
resulting choice between practical-policy evaluation and mechanism identification.
No selected paper establishes an expected accuracy gain for our pinned,
quantized Qwen3.5-9B configuration.

## 5. Recommended scientific question and controlled design

### 5.1 Question, population and scope of the claim

**Question:** Given the same historical source reports, current private shards,
event notice, model and bounded action protocol, what is the effect of exposing
the old correct team conclusion on exact adaptation, and does that effect differ
between actual withdrawal and no change?

The population is newly generated I-01 mathematical worlds with the same declared
withdrawal eligibility, paired with experimenter-supplied, locally permitted
original histories. The old conclusion is correct in the original world. Each
world supplies both event states and both history conditions. Inputs and
history construction are fixed before any model response. Successful recovery
never determines whether a world enters the population.

This tests responses **to supplied historical evidence and conclusions**.
It does not estimate the probability that agents naturally create those
histories, the original retain/clear policy effect, internal anchoring, or
preservation of expensive computation. The model still performs all new
continuation actions and submissions.

### 5.2 Make the historical evidence basis explicit

Use a small legal scripted original exchange as an openly supplied source of
visible records. A simple candidate is a three-call max-exchange history in
which each agent communicates its original indexed shard as ordinary text and
the team reaches the correct original maximum. The exact script must fit the
existing per-agent turns and tokenizer limits. This is a proposed fixture design,
not a claim that such traces have been generated or tested here.

For each agent, construct two separate blocks from **only what that agent could
have seen in that scripted exchange**:

- **F, historical source reports:** original indexed values that were actually
  communicated, their sender, and their original-phase status. These are
  historical reports, not host-certified current truth. The same F bytes appear
  in both conclusion conditions. Current own data remain separate and authoritative.
- **K, old team conclusion:** the original final-answer/submission text, using
  a fixed extraction rule for every world. The K-present condition includes it;
  K-absent omits the entire conclusion block and every duplicate conclusion
  mention. This rule does not selectively erase only values now known to be wrong.

The controlled memory packets are **new representations**, not literal Course-7
transcripts. Source reports and final-answer prose must be separated when the
script is authored, so removing K does not silently rewrite source reports.
Do not leave the old answer in an unredacted private-submission field while
calling the condition conclusion-free. The old maximum can still be derivable
from F; the intervention concerns explicit conclusion exposure, not elimination
of every way to recover that number.

This packet supplies substantial information through historical ordinary
communication. It is therefore a strong controlled starting condition. Giving
both peers the partner's **revised** shard would be a different, full-information
experiment and is excluded. After an actual edit, the unchanged peer still does
not receive the replacement value through the notice. It must obtain adequate
current information from the changed owner. No record is silently refreshed in F,
and neither peer is told the hidden rule that the replacement cannot win.

A compact historical maximum-only F is **not equivalent** to indexed source
records. Once its winning record is withdrawn, that summary may fail to preserve
the remaining candidates. Such a later representation comparison would answer
another useful question, but it should not be mixed into this first design.

### 5.3 Four cells, one primary interaction

| Cell | Current environment | Historical F | Explicit K | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| E0 | Actual adopted-style winning-record withdrawal | Identical original reports | Absent | Adapt with the historical source basis |
| E1 | Same actual withdrawal | Same F | Present | Adapt with the same basis plus the old correct conclusion |
| N0 | No source value changes | Same F | Absent | Recompute/preserve after reopening without K |
| N1 | Same no-change environment | Same F | Present | Preserve when the original conclusion remains correct |

Both event conditions reopen both agents using the same procedural boundary.
The no-change notice uses the same owner/record fields and owner-only current
value policy; it does not supply a policy label or claim that persistence is
correct. The record is the originally winning record in both conditions.
Unlike the existing contract, a no-change boundary also starts a new phase;
that is an explicit prospective amendment, not existing behavior.

Let `Y(w,e,k)` be exact both-agent success within bounds for world `w`, event
`e` and conclusion exposure `k`. Define the benefit of omitting K as:

```text
d_edit(w) = Y(w,edit,absent) - Y(w,edit,present)
d_sham(w) = Y(w,sham,absent) - Y(w,sham,present)
interaction(w) = d_edit(w) - d_sham(w)
```

The primary proposed estimand is the mean world-level interaction. Positive
values indicate that omitting the explicit conclusion is more beneficial after
actual withdrawal than after the sham. Report both component contrasts, all
four success rates, and the complete paired outcome patterns. An interaction
alone could hide poor performance in every cell.

This is an event-dependent **conclusion-exposure effect with matched historical
source evidence**. K also changes text, salience and some length. It is not a
pure psychological anchoring effect. Fixed block placement and a small
predeclared presentation sensitivity check can improve robustness; numeric
filler, invented conclusions and padding should not silently become extra arms.
P13's length control is a precedent, not proof that filler is inert here.

### 5.4 Why the design is preferable for this question

It removes the dependence on finding rare successful natural prefixes, separates
explicit conclusions from an information basis, and gives justified persistence
an equal conceptual role. It also leaves peer uptake, current-source use and
legal final commitment measurable under renewed interaction. Exact scoring is
retained.

Its central cost is artificial competence and a richer supplied history than
many natural Course-7 prefixes. A null could be specific to this representation;
an effect could be caused by the explicit conclusion's presentation. Those are
honest limits, and preferable to attributing an entangled all-history contrast
to memory alone.

### 5.5 A fair competing design: improved natural policy evaluation

If preserving the practical question is more valuable, use one newly frozen
common agent environment, natural one-shot prefixes, and literal retain/clear
sibling starts on a fresh fixed world list. Retain every intended world,
including poor prefixes. Original correctness remains a pre-treatment
descriptive stratum; continuation compliance or success never selects analysis.

Add a no-change event only if the primary claim concerns revision specificity.
That yields another four-continuation design, but its interaction concerns
**whole-history policies**, not conclusions at fixed evidence. It needs prefix
inference and generally more calls than the controlled option. A successful
natural study would offer stronger ecological continuity, while a sparse
correct-prefix subset would still limit a competent-recovery claim.

Prospectively screening natural prefixes is a third option with a conditional
population and charged rejection cost. At Course-7's observed 3/20 rate, merely
obtaining twelve successful prefixes would require about eighty candidate
worlds **in expectation if that rate transferred**. This is an illustration,
not a forecast; screening cost and variability could be prohibitive. A new model
or prompt could change that rate. Screening on recovery outcomes is never valid.

## 6. Separate competence, legality and recovery evidence

### 6.1 One common presentation candidate

The initial candidate should clarify the existing legal choices, private
submission, global objective, finality, current-source authority and event/history
boundaries. Examples should demonstrate action forms without giving the
max-exchange solution sequence. Keep the actual action meanings and strict
rejection rule. If phase labels or rule-specific feedback are included, list
them as ingredients of the candidate.

A combined candidate measures a presentation package. A later claim about which
ingredient helped requires an ablation; a first feasibility study need not
multiply arms merely to attribute every phrase. Hold the selected presentation
constant in all four controlled cells. Histories may not encode the condition
name, current gold answer, partner private submissions, future edit or hidden
eligibility.

Strategy teaching is a separate fallback. If selected, say explicitly that
agents receive a max-exchange strategy. Reopening final submissions, allowing
message-plus-wait, auto-fixing replies or adding more turns would instead change
protocol semantics and require a separate scientific comparison.

### 6.2 Diagnostic levels and causal caution

| Level | Measure | Why it matters | Limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Parsing | JSON, exact fields and types | Separates syntax from action semantics | Does not certify a legal decision |
| Protocol | Valid action, delivery, waiting, private finality | Measures whether the intended exchange can occur | Legal actions can be wrong |
| Current-source use | Own answer below a larger own current value; replacement/record-index confusion | Identifies locally refutable behavior | Does not uniquely identify scanning, attention or imitation |
| Communication | Correct report versus delivered report; acquisition before commitment | Separates content generation from transmission and uptake | Receipt is not proof of belief or sufficiency |
| Team outcome | Both exact submissions, wrong agreement, terminal noncompletion | Preserves the meaningful collaborative endpoint | Agreement alone has no correctness status |
| History response | Old conclusion repeated, warranted persistence under sham | Relates change to exposed history | Copyable-value availability and presentation still matter |
| Effort | All calls/tokens/ready time, including failures | Prevents faster wrong answers appearing efficient | No arbitrary accuracy/cost composite |

Keep the historical `malformed_action` field unchanged; a derived display can
show its more precise legality reason. Any new annotation guide should label
observable events and allow ambiguity. Two human reviewers are useful where
available, but a single-reviewer casebook must be identified as such. No LLM judge
is needed for exact arithmetic, action validation or the primary endpoint.

Claims such as “repairing this message would rescue the team” require a newly
declared intervention and new continuation, not merely a plausible counterfactual
annotation. Such model calls would be charged research execution. They are not
part of this documentation task.

## 7. Fresh development and held-out evaluation

The following is **one prospective bounded option**, not an adopted schedule
or authorization. Counts are worst-case first attempts. A later implementation
plan must establish what existing lifecycle code can be reused for the new
regime; cost arithmetic is not evidence that those cells already work.

### 7.1 Freeze the split before testing

Use three disjoint sets: four original-collaboration development worlds, two
controlled-history development worlds, and twelve held-out confirmation worlds.
All inputs/seeds are new relative to course-1…7 and earlier exposed fixtures.
Exact input/history overlap checks are useful; a new seed alone is not proof of
a new problem. New data reduce local adaptive contamination, not all possible
pretraining exposure or family-level prompt overfitting.

Before any new model call, specify the mathematical generator, owner/first-
speaker assignment, source packet rule, sham event, action presentation, response
limits, model/runtime, scoring, analysis and resource stop rules. Freeze the
confirmation manifest and keep its contents out of candidate development and
example selection. Course-7 traces are development diagnostics forever.

### 7.2 Development, with one candidate

1. **Original collaboration:** Four fresh worlds, one per owner/first-speaker
   cell. Run the frozen Course-7 presentation and one common clarification once
   each, with matched inputs and invocation seeds: at most `4 × 2 × 6 = 48`
   calls. Add four original full-information scalar controls: **52** total.
   This isolates development of the common environment from a policy winner.
2. **Controlled-history smoke:** Two other fresh worlds, each with the four
   proposed continuation cells and original/revised full-information controls:
   at most `2 × (4 × 6 + 2) = 52` calls. These expose packet/source-uptake
   problems; they are insufficient to estimate an interaction reliably.
3. Charge actual-load preparation separately. Using the existing twenty-call
   preparation pattern as an accounting placeholder gives **124** first
   attempts: `52 + 52 + 20`. New agent-level qualification may be needed;
   it must be charged within an explicitly revised allocation, not called free.

Choose or reject the single candidate based on usability, source use and overall
task behavior, without rewarding an omission/retention effect direction. Any
revision after seeing these results creates a new development version. A second
candidate consumes a newly specified allocation and requires untouched validation;
there is no silent trial-until-pass loop.

The scalar controls have different information and prompting from the team
episodes. They diagnose capability in that view; they do not identify a pure
communication contribution by subtraction. If local scanning versus notice
interpretation remains unresolved, reserve a small, separately specified probe
with the same local input/notice presentation and a local-maximum query. Such
extra calls need an explicit allocation; they are not included in the arithmetic
below or a substitute for held-out team outcomes.

For a concrete pre-data decision, require intact deterministic privacy/lifecycle
checks, correct original/revised scalar controls on both controlled development
worlds, and at least three of four sham continuations succeeding. These are
**proposed usefulness criteria**, not statistically established reliability
thresholds. Report every failure. They are deliberately independent of which
conclusion condition benefits under edit. If they are unacceptable for the
research aim, replace them before generating outcomes, not afterward.

Natural original-team accuracy is separately reported, not used to pretend that
scripted histories were naturally acquired. If legality improves while natural
coordination does not, that is an informative behavioral finding. Proceeding
with supplied histories still requires usable controlled continuations; the
new regime does not automatically cure source/finality failures.

### 7.3 Freeze once, then confirm

Twelve untouched worlds give three per owner/first-speaker cell. Each has the
four continuation cells plus two scalar controls: at most **312** scientific
calls, `12 × 26`. A second actual-load preparation placeholder of twenty gives
**332** confirmation first attempts. Combined with development and a proposed
twenty-eight-attempt interruption/requalification reserve:

```text
development:   52 + 52 + 20 = 124
confirmation:  12 × 26 + 20 = 332
reserve:                       28
maximum planned exposure:     484
```

The sixteen remaining calls below a hypothetical new 500 ceiling are unallocated.
They do not authorize more worlds or candidates. These are **new stage
allocations**; they cannot be inserted into Course-7's frozen 380/60/24/8/28
allocation. Any additional qualification, reload, retry or lost-spending
uncertainty has to fit the newly adopted total and stage caps. The reserve is
not ordinary model-error resampling.

The twenty-call preparation placeholder is based on the existing six state
checks, twelve scalar controls and two envelopes. Its sufficiency for new
memory packets and any changed renderer has not been validated. If additional
mandatory checks exceed the cap, reduce scope prospectively or seek a different
budget; never waive a genuine resource gate to preserve this arithmetic.

Course-7's unused allowance supplies no authority for this campaign. GPU time
cannot be credibly inferred from its early-terminating 34.5-minute execution:
a more competent agent may use more calls or longer responses. Qualify the exact
new prompt bound on the actual host and forecast from measured continuation cost
before authorizing the fixed confirmation scope.

### 7.4 Fair assignment and accounting

Keep all four continuations for a world on one logical device, with fresh
call-owned hidden state. Match per-agent invocation seeds across conditions
without reusing their generated replies. Randomize/counterbalance cell order
independently of device; both devices must see multiple orders. Interleave
owner/first-speaker blocks over time. Fixed device speed then largely cancels
within world, while order remains estimable rather than perfectly aliased.

The scripted prefix consumes no model calls, but its construction and supplied
information must be disclosed. Full-information controls are diagnostic
physical cost, not hypothetical distributed-policy cost. A no-change condition
still receives and pays for a complete continuation opportunity. Resume only
from exact saved requests/outcomes; do not reuse a response merely because a
seed, world or apparently equivalent state matches.

### 7.5 Analysis and uncertainty

Keep the **world** as the unit. The four condition outcomes, two controls and
agent turns are dependent. Preserve their grouping in any bootstrap, descriptive
table or sensitivity analysis. Repeated seeds, if later authorized, add
within-world sampling information and require a declared clustered estimand;
they do not become new worlds.

Analyze every scheduled cell. A recorded wrong answer or terminal failure is
zero; unresolved/unexecuted evidence is unknown. Report cell coverage and
world-wise bounds for the interaction, respecting its four constituent outcomes.
If all data are present, missingness bounds do not express sampling precision.

Before confirmation, specify one primary interaction and its uncertainty
procedure. A stratified world bootstrap is an interpretable exploratory option,
but twelve worlds and three per stratum can yield degenerate distributions.
The interaction ranges from −2 to +2 per world; it is not a binomial outcome
and not a McNemar table. Show all paired patterns and component contrasts.
Any exact/randomization analysis must correspond to the actual assignment and
null hypothesis; matched seeds alone do not create a randomization test.

For a larger practical two-arm study, the earlier proposal's paired planning
quantity `Var(D) = q - delta²` remains useful, with uncertain discordance `q`.
For this four-cell interaction, plan from the joint outcome distribution and
within-world covariance instead of treating four arms as independent. A small
feasibility study cannot certify equivalence or choose a precise future sample
size from a handful of discordant cases. More bootstrap draws do not solve this.

## 8. Evidence for proceeding, reframing or stopping

These are decisions about the next **independent** stage, never rules for
dropping confirmation worlds or rewriting a completed result. Any numerical
thresholds must be adopted before outcomes and justified by the intended use.

| Confirmation result | Interpretation | Next decision |
|---|---|---|
| Strong scalar/sham performance; actual-edit behavior varies with K | Feasible controlled adaptation; candidate content effect with declared presentation limits | Consider a separately powered replication, with another history wording or event condition |
| Strong performance in all four cells; little K effect | Informative bounded null: explicit old conclusions are not a major bottleneck under this evidence-rich regime | Retain the null; move to natural history or a justified harder reuse problem only for a new question |
| Both edit cells fail despite good scalar/sham outcomes | Changed-peer information acquisition or changed-state action remains a bottleneck | Examine direct observable failures; do not enlarge the same comparison merely to seek a winner |
| Scalar or sham performance remains poor | Basic competence/presentation prevents a clean recovery interpretation | Stop this configuration; consider one separately declared scaffold or model alternative |
| Only grammar/legality improves, while source confusion/finality persists | Engineering usability improved without establishing task competence | Reject legality as a sufficient readiness claim |
| Effect appears only with a particular notice, block position or long filler | Presentation sensitivity, not a stable content-mechanism result | Reframe as an interface finding or require a new independent robustness study |
| Runtime/source/privacy/accounting uncertainty arises | Experimental integrity is unresolved | Stop admission, preserve known and unknown outcomes separately |

A concrete larger-study readiness target could require at least 10/12 revised
scalar controls correct and at least 9/12 sham successes **in each K condition**,
alongside complete traces and integrity evidence. These are proposed feasibility
targets with wide uncertainty, not universal reliability thresholds or evidence
that the population accuracy exceeds those fractions. Check whether edit cells
are at a floor or ceiling before choosing a new precision target. An observed
effect direction, significance threshold or favored policy is not an admission
criterion.

Abandon the proposed *mechanism interpretation* if the conditions cannot hold F
fixed without revealing revised private information, if K cannot be separated
from the source reports by a uniform rule, or if the common environment cannot
use current sources even in the controlled setting. A smaller rigorously reported
failure study is preferable to increasingly extensive host scaffolding disguised
as agent recovery.

## 9. Implementation boundaries and proportionate future checks

| Category | Examples | Scientific status / required next decision |
|---|---|---|
| Engineering/reporting repair | Derived illegal-action labels; broken documentation links; demonstrated persistence defect | Preserve raw outcomes and frozen score; focused deterministic/documentation checks |
| Behavioral intervention | Common prompt/notice/history clarification, rule-specific feedback, constrained decoding | New declared agent condition; development comparison and fresh held-out evaluation |
| Strategy scaffold | Required max-exchange instructions, forced information-before-submit stages | Changes supplied competence/coordination question; separate condition and interpretation |
| New experimental regime | Supplied source/conclusion packets, sham reopening, four-cell interaction | New target population, history policy, schedule, estimand and budget; explicit contract amendment |
| Capability/runtime alternative | New model, thinking budget, quantization or attention backend | Separate qualification and comparison; no attributed prompt effect when jointly changed |
| Scientific contract amendment | Any adopted change to the above normative choices | User decision before implementation/execution; this proposal makes none |

Preparation for a future authorized implementation should reuse the existing
world IDs, two canonical stores, raw-call persistence, bounded failure handling,
exact replay, checkpoint export and read-only reporting wherever their semantics
fit. Four cells may require modest explicit schedule/state additions; reuse is
not proof that the existing two-policy implementation already supports them.
No second authoritative outcome store, generic provenance graph, routing service
or prompt optimizer is justified.

Focused deterministic validation should establish:

1. Independent original/revised maxima and lawful scripted original histories;
   source records in each packet correspond to actual permitted historical views.
2. F is identical across K conditions; all explicit conclusions occur only in K;
   current own sources and owner-only event values are correct; no truth,
   hidden eligibility or condition labels leak.
3. Actual edit and sham apply once; both reopen phases; siblings have independent
   histories/effects and fresh model state; original evidence stays immutable.
4. Invalid actions deliver nothing and consume turns; message/submission/wait
   alternatives, finality, deadlock and known-failure/unknown distinctions survive
   the new schedule and renderer.
5. Exact persisted replies resume without resampling; attempt and cost totals
   reconcile for the new four-cell schedule; quiescent exports remain inspectable.
6. The actual native rendered envelopes fit declared limits, followed—only when
   separately authorized—by current-load resource/state checks on the real GPUs.

These checks are prospective. They were not implemented or executed in this
literature task. Documentation here requires source/link consistency and
preservation checks; GPU validation belongs to a later authorized stage.

## 10. Research contribution and final decision packet

The most defensible thesis direction is a bounded replication/extension:
**controlled historical evidence and formerly correct conclusions under actual
distributed source change**. Its value would come from identifying when
available evidence, inter-agent uptake and irreversible scalar commitments
separate, with all failures and nulls preserved. It does not require a new memory
architecture and should not claim a new recovery principle.

A strong publication would additionally need an independent robustness
condition—such as another model, history representation, or an answer-preserving
real edit—plus precision appropriate to its claim. The present bounded option
does not promise that breadth. Expanding all three at once would obscure
interpretation and exceed the useful scope of the first study.

The concrete decision is therefore between:

- **Recommended mechanism direction:** one common presentation candidate,
  fixed historical source reports with/without the old correct conclusion,
  actual-edit/sham continuations, and an explicitly small held-out feasibility
