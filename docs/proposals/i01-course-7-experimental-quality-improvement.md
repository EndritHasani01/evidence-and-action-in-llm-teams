# I-01 course-7: experimental quality improvement proposal

16 October 2026. **Proposal only.** This document follows the completed
[independent experimental analysis](../validation/i01-course-7-independent-scientific-audit.md).
It adopts no scientific amendment and changes no executable code, prompt,
configuration, evaluation rule or saved outcome. It authorizes no inference,
GPU spending, new cohort, publication or historical-result migration.
[Specification §12](../../PROJECT_SPECIFICATION.md#12-adopted-i-01-course-regime)
continues to govern the existing study.

## 1. Recommended direction

Preserve course-7 as the completed exploratory evaluation of the system that
actually ran. Its poor outcomes are valid data. The next research investment
should be a **small, prospectively bounded study of the collaborative interface
and original-team competence**, followed by a new held-out two-policy study
only if that work establishes a sufficiently informative system.

Do not spend the next budget merely repeating the current twenty-world design,
relaxing another admission rule, making the task mathematically easier, or
switching to a larger model. The evidence supports a more specific priority:
make legal information exchange, private finality, current-source authority and
revision notices intelligible, then establish whether the team uses that
environment well enough for recovery comparisons to be informative.

For the next natural-policy study, retain the practical all-world estimand and
the exact paired outcome. Improve the common agent environment before collecting
new main outcomes. If the thesis instead needs a mechanism claim about revising
a correct solution, adopt a **separate controlled-prefix regime**. A label change
cannot turn course-7 into that experiment. Do not combine a new action interface,
new model, controlled history and new task family in one undiagnosable redesign.

### 1.1 Why this recommendation follows from the audit

The decisive evidence is not simply 10% team accuracy. It is the combination of:

- Only three correct original teams, and two recovery-success worlds whose
  original history consisted only of mutual waits.
- Sixty action violations among 206 team calls, although all scientific JSON
  and field types were valid.
- Ten original main episodes with no delivered message, immediate recovery
  finalization, and explicit requests to “adjust” after irreversible submission.
- Locally refutable recovery submissions and notice-as-partner-submission claims,
  including on the first clear call with empty history.
- Lower retained effort, but no greater team success; obsolete answers disappear
  under clear without reliable revised answers replacing them.
- Consistent canonical evidence and no demonstrated scoring/delivery defect.

These observations favor targeted design and measurement work. They do not
justify inventing a software bug, asserting an internal reasoning cause, or
treating every invalid action as an infrastructure failure.

## 2. Choose the scientific target before changing the system

There are three coherent targets. Each can be valuable, but they require
different evidence and should not share an ambiguous primary claim.

| Target | Population and comparison | What it can establish | Main limitation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Practical policy effect in an imperfect natural team** | All fixed worlds and naturally generated prefixes; retain versus clear | Total effect of visible-history policy in a declared agent environment | Mixes good/bad histories, protocol effects and information availability |
| **B. Recovery after a competent natural collaboration** | Prospectively specified naturally successful-prefix population, or all worlds under a demonstrably competent fixed system | Policy behavior after an actual correct distributed solution | Screening changes the population and has physical cost; success may be rare |
| **C. Controlled information/history mechanism** | Experimenter-defined, valid visible histories, then randomized/matched retention intervention | Effect of specified historical content under controlled starting conditions | Synthetic history is not evidence of naturally acquired collaboration or spontaneous memory use |

**Default recommendation:** develop a more interpretable environment, then run
target A with all intended worlds, using a prospectively checked system capable
of producing substantially more informative prefixes. Keep its correct-prefix
analysis secondary. This preserves the current practical question while making
the measurement less dominated by initial coordination failure.

**If the required thesis claim is specifically about updating a correct shared
solution**, choose B or C explicitly before designing the next main run. C is
the more direct, resource-efficient mechanism test when natural successful
prefixes remain rare. It should be reported alongside the natural exploratory
study as a different regime, not pooled into its estimate.

This decision has greater scientific impact than choosing temperature or model
size. The study cannot simultaneously promise natural behavior, guaranteed
competent prefixes and no selection/scaffolding assumptions.

## 4. Immediate scientific reporting work

### 4.1 Preserve the original result and its denominators

Use the analysis's primary table unchanged: 2/20 versus 2/20, one discordance in
each direction, seventeen neither-success, all intended worlds included. Keep
the frozen bootstrap and its caveat, the three-world correct-prefix denominator,
full control errors, all invalid outputs and recorded effort. Do not promote
agent-level accuracy, consensus, protocol-valid-only success or old-answer
avoidance into a replacement primary endpoint.

Distinguish four states in every report: correct terminal answer; incorrect
terminal answer; terminal noncompletion; unknown/unexecuted evidence. A database
`completed` attempt means the provider returned, not that the action was legal
or the task succeeded. Missingness bounds `[0,0]` mean no missing primary data,
not certainty that the population effect is zero.

Label the old-value comparison as observed obsolete-answer repetition with
different old-value availability. Report that clear removes access to the
withdrawn unique value through history and current data. It is useful behavior
evidence, but not an independent proof of debiasing.

### 4.2 Produce a small, reproducible failure account

Use three layers, with explicit denominators:

1. **Deterministic record facts:** syntax, field types, action legality, delivered
   message count, terminal reason, submission equality, locally refutable value,
   exact old-action repetition and known effort. These need no LLM judge.
2. **Trace-supported descriptions:** explicit plan to revise an already final
   submission; treating a record ID as a partner answer; message/submission
   contradiction; visible adoption of a supplied number. Cite exact calls and
   give coders the actual permitted input.
3. **Mechanism hypotheses:** anchoring, imitation, attention to salient notice
   fields, failed scanning or other internal explanations. Keep these uncertain
   unless a dedicated intervention discriminates among them.

For a thesis appendix, two human reviewers can independently inspect all 69
team episodes; 206 actions is manageable. Freeze a short coding guide first.
Allow multiple observable labels, an “insufficient evidence” category and
specific call references. Report agreement, disagreements and adjudication;
do not treat a high agreement statistic as validation of an internal mechanism.
Branch labels can be hidden where possible, but content itself often reveals
history condition, so complete blinding cannot honestly be promised.

If a second reviewer is unavailable, present single-reviewer descriptive cases
and deterministic counts, without claiming independent human validation. This
document and its companion do not add canonical human annotation rows.

### 4.3 Repair nomenclature without rewriting history

A future derived display should say “illegal action” for the 58 message-plus-wait
and two empty-nonwait replies, while preserving the recorded `malformed_action`
field. Show JSON validity, field schema and action legality separately. Explain
that 28 malformed-labelled episodes contain 60 invalid calls and that labels
overlap. This is a reporting correction, not a more permissive validator.

Clarify the generator's balanced owner conditioning in a prospective contract
note, retaining original rejection records. Nothing in this audit supports
regenerating course-7's inputs. A future scheduling fix must likewise leave
the old device/order assignment untouched.

## 5. First development intervention: clarify the environment, keep its mathematics

### 5.1 One minimal candidate before a model sweep

Start with **one** common presentation candidate against the frozen baseline.
Its scope should be narrow enough that a result can be interpreted:

- Show unambiguous legal action examples, especially message-only versus
  wait-only. Use actual JSON null rather than a phrase that could be read as
  the string `"null"`. Make the mutually exclusive fields explicit.
- Explain that every agent submits the **global** answer, submission is private
  unless messaged, and finishing prevents later correction. A proposal in
  message prose is not a provisional `submit` action.
- Render the source revision as an environment notice, with clearly named
  changed-owner and record-index fields. State uniformly that the notice is
  not a partner message or partner submission. Preserve owner-only access to
  the replacement value.
- Separate the current authoritative shard from the history presentation.
  Mark historical entries' phase context without altering their literal text.
  Both policies should receive the same current-source/revision instructions;
  treatment labels and hidden eligibility rules remain absent.
- If feedback is changed, identify the exact violated syntactic/action rule
  without revealing correctness, truth, hidden data or a preferred answer.

These are proposed changes to the **agent environment**. They may preserve the
mathematical problem and retention transformation while still changing behavior
and therefore the experimental condition. They require a new declared version;
they are not a retrospective bug fix. Keep the old frozen source intact.

There is no recommendation to expose the second-largest value, tell peers that
the replacement can never win, give a host-computed local maximum, disclose
private partner submissions, or erase only the harmful parts of retained history.
Those changes would alter information or intervention semantics substantially.

### 5.2 Separate clarification from algorithm teaching

The native I-01 description gives more aggregation guidance than course-7's
compact prompt. A general reminder that all agents need the global answer
clarifies the endpoint. Prescribing “exchange local maxima, compare, broadcast,
then submit” supplies a strategy. It may be a good **scaffolded** condition, but
it changes the degree of spontaneous coordination being measured.

Test clarification first. If it produces legal actions but persistent local-only
finalization, a separate explicitly scaffolded condition becomes well motivated.
Do not claim the latter demonstrates autonomous discovery of a coordination
algorithm. Keep a deterministic max-exchange baseline as a correctness and
feasibility reference, not as a replacement for model-generated answers.

### 5.3 Alternatives and why they are not first choice

| Alternative | Potential benefit | Scientific cost / reason to defer |
|---|---|---|
| Discriminated action kind or separate message/submit/wait tools | Makes incompatible combinations harder to express | New action interface; may still finalize wrong local values; requires fresh qualification |
| Grammar/schema-constrained decoding | Could eliminate structural/action violations if the grammar encodes semantic alternatives | Changes the sampling distribution and available actions; JSON-only constraints alone miss the actual problem |
| Allow message-and-wait | Fits the model's frequently expressed intent | Changes waiting/delivery/scheduling semantics; cannot be applied to old rejected messages |
| Automatically change invalid wait to non-wait | Cheap apparent gain | Repairs the model's action and changes treatment exposure; reject for historical evidence |
| More specific feedback | Directly addresses repeated illegal combinations | Behaviorally informative intervention; does not establish global reasoning competence |
| More turns | May help some horizons | Many failures submit immediately or legally deadlock; more budget can preserve the same error |
| Reopen submissions on later messages | Permits correction after early commitment | Changes a central protocol constraint and endpoint; could mask finality planning failure |
| Force ordered send/compare/submit stages | Ensures opportunities for coordination | A stronger scaffold and different agent task; useful baseline, not natural coordination |

If clarification fails, choose **one** next alternative based on the residual
trace pattern. Do not combine all of them and attribute any improvement to one
ingredient. Strict validation should remain the arbiter of effects in each
declared regime; ordinary failures should remain charged outcomes.

## 6. Measure the capabilities the experiment actually needs

### 6.1 Keep runtime gates strict; distinguish scientific usefulness

Source binding, privacy, finite scores, fresh state, durable raw replies/effects,
bounded termination, reconciled spending, actual headroom and sufficient session
margin remain hard requirements. Poor mathematical performance cannot waive them.

Scalar qualification remains useful for loading/format/elementary capability.
It should not be described as full collaborative readiness. Add a small fixed
development battery exercising the permitted actions and the following behaviors:

| Capability | Diagnostic evidence | What not to infer |
|---|---|---|
| Legal message and wait use | Exact raw actions and delivered effects | Valid JSON alone is enough |
| Private finality | Does the first submission occur after relevant partner evidence? Does a message promise impossible later adjustment? | A correct lucky first submission proves coordination |
| Aggregation | Do agents compare correct local values and both submit the global result? | Equal submissions prove they used correct evidence |
| Current source authority | Response not below a larger own current value; use of changed record correctly described | One correct scalar proves reliable revision handling |
| Revision-notice interpretation | No nonexistent partner submission inferred on an empty-history first call | Absence of the old answer proves successful updating |
| History use | Literal action/message repetition, stale values, retained useful facts | Observable repetition establishes an internal anchoring mechanism |

Some descriptions require human review; retain deterministic fields alongside
them. A single arbitrary numerical “coordination score” would hide distinctions
the audit has shown to matter.

### 6.2 Admission rules should follow the chosen target

For target A, ordinary failures remain outcomes in the final fixed main cohort.
Do not stop, replace worlds or retune because the treatment effect is unfavorable.
Development may establish that a proposed system is scientifically uninformative;
then report that feasibility result and decide on a new version before main.
This is different from repeatedly relaxing gates within a frozen execution.

For target B, a successful original collaboration may define eligibility, but
the screening mechanism must be explicit: fixed prospective world stream,
screening cap, all rejected/failed prefixes retained, accounting for every
attempt, selection before either recovery response, and a stated conditional
population. It cannot be presented as all-world recovery performance. Never
screen using retain/clear outcomes or choose the most convincing prefix among
multiple stochastic attempts after inspecting its continuations.

A minimum baseline-usefulness target is a scientific choice. For example, if a
future twenty-world study needs about fifteen correct prefixes to examine that
subset, a development target near 75% original-team success is intelligible.
It is not a universal threshold or a reason to reinterpret this run's 15%.
Small development samples must report uncertainty; an apparent 9/12 success
rate does not certify a population reliability of 75%.

## 7. Stronger identification without an oversized factorial study

### 7.1 Keep the natural two-arm comparison if the claim is practical

Use one fixed common model/prompt/action environment and a fixed new world list.
Generate one natural prefix per world and retain its actual good/bad outcome.
Run both siblings from that exact prefix, on the same logical device, with
matched per-agent invocation seeds and independent continuation calls. Preserve
all intended worlds and all failed outcomes. This remains the simplest design
for a total practical retain/clear effect.

Improve schedule assignment prospectively: generate physical sibling order
independently of GPU slot, counterbalance it across owner/first-speaker blocks,
and interleave blocks over time. With five worlds per block, exact 50/50 order
balance is impossible within every cell; use a declared 2/3 allocation with
complementary cells and record the random seed. Each device should see both
orders. Do not add extra worlds just to make a perfectly rectangular table
unless a new sample size is scientifically justified.

Both policies should retain the same source/inference settings. Use actual
device UUIDs and attempt timing boundaries to diagnose physical effects. The
schedule need not become a general experimental-assignment framework.

### 7.2 If updating a correct solution is central, use a controlled prefix honestly

A compact alternative supplies both agents with valid, explicitly scripted
visible histories produced by a legal max-exchange protocol, then applies the
same source revision and retain/clear transformation. The original maximum and
relevant remote facts can be guaranteed present in the history by construction.
The evaluator never repairs a model's prior answer; the entire regime is openly
defined as externally supplied history before any model response.

This directly addresses course-7's small correct-prefix denominator and can
reduce wasted prefix inference. It estimates response to a controlled visible
history, not recovery of a naturally generated solution. Check that the injected
history is locally permitted, literal, ordered, internally valid and compatible
with the endpoint. Do not claim hidden reasoning was acquired or retained.

Begin with the same two history policies. Add one selective condition only if
the intended claim needs it. For example, preserving verified remote input facts
while withholding the old final conclusion could test whether stale conclusion
text adds harm beyond access to useful information. The contrast would still
change text length and presentation unless controlled; matching length with
arbitrary filler may introduce its own effects. Do not call such a condition
“pure anchoring” without a carefully specified causal contrast.

### 7.3 Add a no-revision control only for a correction-specific claim

The existing two arms cannot distinguish an effect of clearing history in
general from an effect specifically caused by a factual correction. A matched
no-change continuation would permit the contrast:

```text
(clear − retain after revision) − (clear − retain without revision)
```

That requires two additional continuations per prefix and a declared same-answer
control event. It changes the estimand and consumes additional calls. It is
well motivated for a correction-specific thesis, but unnecessary for a narrow
practical withdrawal-policy question. Choose this extension **instead of** an
unfocused set of mechanism arms if revision specificity is the primary claim.
A no-change control cannot retrospectively be reconstructed from course-7's
revision outcomes.

### 7.4 Do not add harder task families before establishing the basic contrast

I-01 is cheap and exactly evaluable, which makes it useful for development.
Its tiny reusable computation limits a broad retain-work benefit claim. Once
the collaborative environment works, a later task with meaningful reusable
derived work could test a different regime. Select it for an explicit theoretical
reason and exact evaluator, not to obtain a favorable retention effect.

Replacing maximum with a harder task immediately would mix task difficulty,
protocol competence and memory needs. Replacing it with an even easier scalar
task could produce ceiling effects without demonstrating real coordination.
Task expansion belongs after an interpretable I-01 result or an explicitly
changed research question.

## 8. Proportionate validation plan

The following is a **concrete prospective option**, not a new adopted schedule.
Its purpose is to bound development and preserve a meaningful held-out check.
New master seeds, input lists, configuration versions and spending scope require
explicit adoption. Course-7's unused hard allowance is not its budget.

### 8.1 Stage V0: read-only and deterministic preparation

Use the audited failures as diagnostic fixtures, not success demonstrations.
Before any future model call:

1. State the selected target, primary endpoint, failure/missingness rules and
   useful-prefix requirement. Save the baseline and one candidate presentation.
2. Check exact truth and legal scripted max-exchange completion within existing
   turn limits. Include waiting deadlock, finality, invalid delivery and notice
   privacy cases already implicated by the audit.
3. Verify both siblings receive identical current sources and notice rules;
   only declared retained history differs. Check phase labels preserve literal
   text and reveal no hidden information.
4. Verify parser/renderer/native template, full input/output bound, fresh state,
   persistence, exact resume, headroom and accounting only where changes affect
   them. Reuse valid prerequisite evidence rather than rerunning a large suite
   unrelated to the change.
5. Freeze a held-out validation list before testing candidates. Keep all exposed
   course-2…7 worlds and their shared seeds outside a claim of new validation.

No model is necessary for these checks. An improvement in a scripted oracle
fixture does not establish a model improvement.

### 8.2 Stage V1: small fixed development comparison

Use six new development worlds for original-team comparisons between the old
presentation and one candidate. Execute each once under the prespecified seed
schedule, preserving paired world inputs. At six calls per episode this costs
at most **72 team calls**. Add one full-information original control per world
if scalar-versus-collaborative diagnosis is needed: **six calls**.

Inspect legal action rates, terminal states, both-agent global success,
message/submission contradictions, first-finalization evidence and effort.
Do not select by a retain/clear effect: this first comparison has no recovery
policy endpoint. Its purpose is to distinguish interface/coordination behavior.

Then use four additional fresh development worlds, one per owner/first-speaker
cell, for complete candidate prefix/retain/clear/revised-control traces:
at most **4 × 19 = 76 calls**. These are diagnostic examples of revision and
history behavior, not a precise estimate of treatment superiority. Candidate
selection may use these development results, but their outcomes never enter
the later held-out estimate.

If fresh preparation is needed, the current six readiness + twelve scalar
qualification + two envelope pattern costs at most twenty first calls.
Thus this illustrative development allocation is **174 first attempts**
(`72 + 6 + 76 + 20`). It is an upper bound, not a forecast or spending target.
Any retry, reload probe or alternative candidate consumes an explicit reserve;
there is no unrecorded trial-until-success loop.

### 8.3 Stage V2: freeze once, then held-out confirmation

After development, select exactly one candidate—or reject it—and freeze all
prompt, feedback, model, settings, source and schedule choices. A proposed
confirmation cohort of **twelve new worlds**, balanced three per owner/first
cell, has at most **228 scientific first attempts** (`12 × 19`). A new session
may need another twenty preparation calls. Combined with V1, the illustrative
campaign uses at most **422 first attempts**; reserving twenty for explicitly
eligible interruptions/reloads yields **442**. This fits beneath a separately
approved 500 cap, but leaves no automatic authority to add a twenty-world main
experiment afterward.

The allocation assumes one shared resident model-load pair per development or
confirmation phase under the declared common runtime. Additional independently
required preparation is not free; it must be charged and may require a smaller
prospectively chosen scope or a newly authorized budget. Never skip a genuine
qualification or headroom requirement to fit this arithmetic.

Twelve confirmation worlds can validate behavior and reveal a new floor; they
cannot establish high reliability or a modest treatment advantage precisely.
Even twelve perfect prefixes have a one-sided 95% exact reliability lower bound
of only about 77.9%, since `0.05^(1/12) ≈ 0.779`. Label it a held-out feasibility
confirmation if that is its purpose, not a definitive publication-sized effect
study. If research requirements instead demand a full twenty-world main study,
choose a smaller development allocation or a separately authorized larger
campaign **before** generation; do not quietly repurpose reserves.

For a concrete competent-team usefulness decision, the researchers could
prospectively require at least nine correct original teams among twelve,
complete interpretable traces and intact runtime evidence, while reporting the
wide exact uncertainty. The rationale would be to seek roughly fifteen usable
prefixes in a later twenty-world design, not to certify 75% population accuracy.
This is an explicit proposed scientific choice and must be accepted or replaced
before unseen outcomes. No recovery-effect direction or policy winner belongs
in that decision rule.

If the confirmation fails, preserve its complete result and do not tune on it
while retaining the label “held out.” Either stop with a feasibility conclusion
or adopt a new development cycle and new validation inputs. A candidate that
only suppresses invalid actions but leaves source confusion/finality failures
is not demonstrated to improve the recovery experiment.

### 8.4 What would count as improvement?

Judge the common environment on a vector of outcomes:

| Dimension | Positive evidence | Insufficient evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Execution integrity | Exact permitted views, unchanged truth, committed outcomes and reconciled spending | Merely finishing every notebook cell |
| Interface competence | Fewer illegal actions on new traces with the same strict meaning | A host sanitizer converts bad replies to actions |
| Coordination | More correct original teams, explicit information exchange before finality, less locally refutable behavior | More messages or greater consensus alone |
| Recovery interpretability | Enough relevant prefixes, correct revised source use, trace-supported policy differences | Old values disappear but new answers stay wrong |
| Reproducibility | Frozen source/settings; all outcomes retained; independent read-only reconstruction | Identical re-executions counted as independent samples |
| Resource efficiency | Accuracy, completion and ready time reported jointly | Faster wrong finalization or an arbitrary combined accuracy/cost score |

Do not require retain and clear to differ in a preferred direction. An improved
experiment may still produce a null result, provided the system and controls
make that result scientifically informative.

## 9. Inference and sample-size planning for the next study

Keep paired world-level analysis, with siblings and prefix dependency together.
Calls, agents and repeated original/revised controls are not independent sample
units. Separate new-world variability from repeated-generation variability:
if stochastic repetitions are added, define their role and average/cluster
within world according to a prospectively declared estimand.

Preserve success/noncompletion and report failed effort. Keep fixed denominators
and completion bounds for unknowns. Human-coded mechanisms and exploratory
subgroups should have a clearly limited role; a large collection of subgroup
tests cannot compensate for sparse primary discordances.

Plan sample size from a meaningful paired effect or desired precision after
development establishes the relevant outcome distribution. For paired binary
outcomes, if `q` is the probability of discordance and `delta` the mean
clear-minus-retain effect, then `Var(D)=q−delta^2`. Near zero, an approximate
95% half-width `h` suggests `n ≈ 1.96^2 q/h^2`, before accounting for strata and
small-sample behavior. For `q=0.1…0.5`, half-width 0.10 suggests roughly 39…193
worlds; half-width 0.05 suggests roughly 154…769. These are planning illustrations,
not accepted sample sizes or a power guarantee. Course-7's two discordances
are too few to estimate `q` reliably.

Use lightweight deterministic simulation of the declared paired model and
estimator to inspect interval coverage and power over a plausible grid, including
floor/ceiling and degenerate cells. Choose one primary contrast and interval
procedure before the new main run. Preserve the original exploratory bootstrap
for course-7; a new exact or score interval is an amendment for future analysis,
not a post hoc way to make the old result stronger. A paired sign test assesses
direction among discordances and does not by itself estimate the magnitude of
the total success difference.

Increasing the number of empirical bootstrap draws cannot create new information
when there are only two observed nonzero differences. Improving the number of
relevant prefixes and the validity of the contrast takes priority over a larger
resampling computation.

## 10. Model capability, backend checks and computational efficiency

### 10.1 Escalate capability interventions only when diagnosis warrants them

The controls show real scalar errors, but the distributed failure includes
legal finalization of known-wrong local answers. A bigger or thinking model
could help and remains an empirical option, not a diagnosed solution. The five
historical thinking calls are incomplete and cannot rank modes fairly. No
within-run precision/model comparison identifies quantization as the cause.

If the clarified environment still fails scalar and coordinated controls,
consider one fixed alternative at a time: explicit algorithmic scaffold,
different supported decoding condition, reasoning budget, or model. State
whether the goal is a practical best-performing system or an explanatory
ablation; these imply different comparisons. Use development inputs for choice,
then freeze before new validation. Charge all attempts and requalify resource,
privacy/state and prompt bounds. Do not use benchmark reputation as evidence
that the actual bounded environment is capable.

The pinned model card is useful configuration context, but its recommended
penalties and long reasoning budgets do not automatically fit this protocol
or T4 budget. It provides no causal explanation of course-7's failures.
[Pinned Qwen model card](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.5-9B/blob/c202236235762e1c871ad0ccb60c8ee5ba337b9a/README.md).

### 10.2 Keep numerical verification narrow and useful

Before relying on a changed attention/quantization backend for a stronger study,
compare selected fixed requests against an independently trusted reference
where feasible, examining logits or bounded numerical error as well as final
outputs. Sampling can magnify small logit differences; exact free-generation
token equality is neither a universal requirement nor sufficient proof of
mathematical equivalence. Preserve finite-score, fresh-state and model-load
audits separately from capability outcomes.

No backend rewrite is presently justified as a repair for course-7. Its missing
recurrent fast path may affect speed, but the run has ample headroom, short
scientific contexts and no timeout/truncation evidence. Installing new kernels
into the frozen study to “fix accuracy” would add an uncontrolled condition.

### 10.3 Efficiency should serve an informative experiment

Keep resident weights, same-device siblings, bounded batches, quiescent exports
and exact replay. Avoid repeated whole-study qualification solely to get the
same known answers. Any permitted reuse of preparation must be explicit and
load/source-bound; do not infer permission to skip new-load headroom checks.

A future minimal resource record can add timestamped inference boundaries,
device utilization samples and actual operator allocation/release observations
to the existing receipts. No monitoring service is needed. Separate ready-model
time, load/setup, export overhead, wall time and billed allocation. Use current
measurements to forecast conservatively; do not extrapolate this 34.5-minute
run as a guaranteed duration for a more competent or verbose system.

Shorter prompts/outputs may reduce effort, but a new lower output cap could
create truncation and change action quality. Batching scientific calls could
change RNG/numerical behavior. Treat performance changes as separately verified
implementation conditions and preserve the experiment's source binding.

## 11. Decisions that should remain explicit

| Decision | Recommended default | Why it needs a conscious choice |
|---|---|---|
| Scientific target | A for the next natural study; C if mechanism identification is required | Determines whether weak prefixes are part of the population or a feasibility limitation |
| First candidate | Minimal common interface/source-notice clarification | Most directly motivated by traces; preserves mathematics and avoids a model sweep |
| Strategy teaching | Defer; separate scaffolded condition if needed | Could solve coordination by supplying the solution procedure |
| Action-space redesign | Defer until clarification diagnosis | Changes available behavior and sampling, not only wording |
| Current dataset | Preserve all outcomes and original scoring | Prevents evidence contamination |
| New validation population | Fresh fixed worlds/seeds, distinct from exposed development | Repeated identical requests are not independent validation |
| Prefix competence requirement | Tie to intended inference, freeze before new data | A target-A study and a competent-recovery study have different admission needs |
| Additional scientific arm | Only one motivated control when needed: sham event or selective history | Prevents an infeasible factorial study without a clear estimand |
| Sample size | Plan after development, from meaningful effect/precision | More current-system worlds alone would not resolve confounding |
| Compute allocation | New explicit bounded campaign with development charged | Course-7's unused allowance is not permission to expand |

The smallest defensible next step is therefore a declared common-environment
development comparison with fixed inputs, one candidate, strict recorded
outcomes and an untouched held-out confirmation set. A subsequent natural or
controlled-prefix study should be chosen for the claim it can identify. Until
those choices and resource authorization are made, the deliverable remains this
analysis-backed proposal; implementation and scientific-contract changes stop
here.
