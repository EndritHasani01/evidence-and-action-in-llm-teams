## Intended outcome

The validated offline pipeline produces the actual report and supporting outputs, with claims limited to audited evidence and declared populations.

## Scope and boundaries

- For main evidence, run M4's frozen estimands, uncertainty, diagnostics, costs, repeatability and case rules. Show all focal populations, family/world contrasts, paired coverage and missingness bounds, inactive Q, capability controls, annotation intersections and assumptions.
- Preserve the planned grouped exhibits and source-linked unaltered case excerpts. Never tune cases, annotation rules, endpoints or the boundary policy after seeing results.
- Keep package effects, observed communication paths, deterministic mathematical sufficiency and semantic causation distinct.
- Interpret equal revised/sham effects as potentially generic cleanup; retain subgroup cancellation, receipt benefits without interaction, delay costs, ceiling damage/cost differences, poor capability, wide intervals, negative effects and unstable repeats.
- Identify these as SILO-derived revision-task results, not native benchmark reproduction or evidence that published SILO results are wrong. Preserve exact source attribution.
- Verify precise cited source/version claims before publication, including SILO, CIR, ICR, StateAuditor, DBSP, REVISE and applicable archive caveats. Do not make an exhaustive novelty or superiority claim unsupported by matching interfaces.
- State the limits: one announced edit, synchronous reliable communication, four peers, one accepted quantized non-thinking model, selected small tasks, public hints/permitted centralization and the observed horizon. No broader asynchronous/adversarial/larger-team/frontier-model claims.
- Limited route: issue an explicitly incomplete report with coverage/bounds, or a feasibility report with pilot failures/resources and absent main results. Development rows never substitute for the main population.

## Completion criteria

- Every empirical statement traces to audited artifacts with its environment, coverage and denominator.
- Synthetic validation, pilot evidence and main outcomes are clearly distinguished.
- Missing/unexecuted work and capability/precision/causal limits remain visible; no completed-study claim if execution is incomplete.
- Report, tables, figures, selected cases and source/version review are ready for local release verification, without publication.

## Validation and evidence

Reproduce outputs using the accepted analysis settings, trace headline claims and cases back to calls/messages/worlds, check all secondary/focal populations and uncertainty, and audit the stated limits against §10. Verify reference claims against the actual cited versions. For limited reports check absent main results and unknown coverage cannot be mistaken for negative model outcomes.
