Apply the 25% main margin and adopted 8/2/2-hour development/setup/interruption reserves within the proposed 30-hour ceiling; report spending separately without double-counting setup.
- Keep failed configurations/reruns within the shared budget and disclose them. Do not pool validity or timing across incompatible models/templates/environments/caps. No main outcome may guide scope, prompt or policy selection.
