## Intended outcome

An ABS/IIS course report presents audited empirical or explicitly limited feasibility evidence with reproducible exhibits, precise interpretations and traceable claims.

## Scope and boundaries

- Derive the complete M4 report from audited records: both primaries, all secondary populations and companions, family/world contrasts, uncertainty, fixed-population missingness bounds, paired coverage, inactive treatments, trajectories/annotations, policy costs, capability and five-world repeatability limitations. Use frozen cases/excerpts and the seven/eight-exhibit organization, with supporting tables.
- Show accepted model/quantization/size/caps/horizon, actual execution coverage, pilot screens, resource forecast versus spending, allocation uncertainty, deviations and negative/inconclusive results. Equal event effects, cancellation, ceiling performance, poor capability and broad intervals remain interpretable.
- Treat 20 worlds/four per family, eight-world focal scopes and five conditional continuation repeats as a small exploratory study. Repeats add no independent clusters; these five task families are not a random task sample. Smaller models/caps/instances alter the regime and do not close the original thesis obligations.
- Distinguish policy package effects, observed communication paths, mathematical sufficiency and semantic causation. Do not claim isolated hidden anchoring, arbitrary external-tool robustness, a causal message effect, an achievable hindsight policy winner or native SILO reproduction. Preserve controlled announced-change/synchronous/private-information limits and all specification exclusions.
- Verify precise cited source/version claims and attribution for SILO-derived tasks, CIR, ICR, StateAuditor, DBSP and REVISE before any publication; preserve relevant provenance/licenses. Align the course framing with known ABS/IIS requirements without inventing acronyms, rubric or required external delivery.
- If feasibility failed or main execution stopped, write the corresponding limited report. Label CPU/synthetic validation, actual model smoke, development and main evidence separately; unavailable outcomes are not negative results and a missing freeze is not fabricated.

## Completion criteria

- Every empirical statement/table/case can be traced to audited artifacts and the frozen analysis, with all required populations, intervals, coverage and caveats represented.
- A claims/source audit passes and the report describes the actual complete or limited course outcome. No course result is presented as completion of thesis tasks.
- Report, figures, supporting tables and limitations are ready for local reproduction and authorized delivery.

## Validation and evidence

Regenerate report inputs offline, check case/annotation/excerpt provenance and numerically cross-check major claims against tidy tables. Review citations against primary sources where needed and test the report's completeness label against the audit. Retain report paths and the final claims review.
