## Intended outcome

The verified course deliverable reaches its explicitly agreed destination, with an accurate completion or limited-outcome handoff and preserved thesis continuation work.

## Scope and boundaries

- Use the already reproduced local bundle/report as the concrete deliverable. The default course outcome is local handoff; do not invent a required public publication, portal upload or message to someone else. Confirm any external destination/action within its explicit authorization before sending or publishing.
- For authorized external delivery, use only the approved version/scope and verify the destination/result. Prepare release details and content first; public publication, private course submission and a local report remain distinct actions.
- Record accepted configuration, actual complete/partial/feasibility coverage, allocation costs/uncertainties, reproduction location and remaining work. Update the course task/index evidence and existing plan living progress during implementation; preserve thesis todos, artifacts and unmet original gates.
- A terminal-feasibility or incomplete-main report can be handed off without passing the failed gate. Keep M5/main tasks unfinished and label the scientific outcome limited; do not convert reporting completion into a completed empirical study.

## Completion criteria

- The adopted local deliverable has been handed off, or the explicitly requested external action has a verified destination/version/receipt. If external approval is still pending, retain the ready local result and leave that delivery scope unfinished.
- Final status agrees with actual schedule, audits, local reproduction and delivery evidence; no unrequested external action or unsupported publication/completion claim occurs.
- The course handoff clearly distinguishes follow-up course work and original thesis obligations without rewriting either population or history.

## Validation and evidence

Check the delivered version against the reproduced local bundle, verify links/access or the applicable destination receipt, and audit final task counts/coverage labels. Record the action, authorized scope, destination and version; no new model run is required.
