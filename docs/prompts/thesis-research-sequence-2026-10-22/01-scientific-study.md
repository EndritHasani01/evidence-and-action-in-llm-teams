## Make an independent scientific choice

Reconsider the original thesis matrix, the six-cell proposal and the later
four-cell recommendation. Do not simply endorse the latest document.

In particular:

1. Identify the most valuable unanswered question supported by the course
   failures. Source/owner interpretation, information use, coordination and
   formal commitments all matter. The absence of obsolete-maximum submissions
   means an old-answer anchoring explanation is not already established.
2. Decide whether the main comparison should concern practical recovery
   policies, the value of historical evidence, explicit old commitments, or a
   different, better-supported question. Explain what each candidate would and
   would not identify. A small effect on a contrived cue is not automatically
   the best thesis just because the comparison is clean.
3. Compare supplied histories with naturally generated histories. Specify the
   population and the cost of acquiring it. Do not select successful natural
   prefixes or repair historical model outputs. Explain whether a small bridge
   can support the intended claim or only illustrate a transfer limitation.
4. Examine the maximum task's information structure. Withdrawal below the
   remaining maximum can permit a deletion shortcut when full old reports are
   available. Compare meaningful edit types and show when a new private value
   must reach the other agent. Use worked examples and distinguish evaluator
   knowledge from each peer's allowed view.
5. Compare staying with I-01 against the smallest worthwhile task alternative
   or extension. Assess scientific value, capability risk, independent sample
   size and implementation reuse. Do not revive the full original matrix by
   default or add a task merely for breadth.
6. Select the necessary controls, policies, interaction rules and final-action
   endpoint. Distinguish no change from an actual answer-preserving edit. State
   exactly how a new phase reopens agents, including in no-change episodes.
   Keep authority, privacy, literal retention and finality explicit.

Use the literature critically. Search independently where an unresolved choice
could change the recommendation; consult actual full texts and relevant
appendices, not just abstracts. Verify versions and primary references. Do not
expand the bibliography merely to increase its size. Separate researchers'
findings, their interpretations and your own transfer hypotheses.

## Resolve statistics, models and feasibility

The intended model systems are **Qwen3.5-9B and IBM Granite 4.2-8B**. Research
their relevant capabilities and official configuration/template guidance. Keep
model-card claims separate from accepted behavior in our task. Investigate
whether the chosen protocol is a fair and useful cross-model comparison,
including mode, quantization, decoding, context, output and equal development
opportunities. Do not silently move to a newer checkpoint or easier model.

Choose one primary question and a small number of primary estimands. Define
their population, unit, weights and sign. Explain what a direct recovery effect
and a revision-minus-no-change interaction each mean. Preserve all cell means,
paired gains/losses and the failed-versus-unexecuted distinction.

Compare more independent worlds with more trials per world at fixed budget.
Choose an exact target sample, any fallback, repeat allocation and stopping
rules. Give analytic precision/power sensitivities over defensible variance or
discordance ranges; do not present an assumed effect or pilot variance as known.
State which small effects the study cannot resolve and what conclusions remain
useful if results are null, mixed, floor-limited or uncertain. Define the
multiplicity and degenerate-interval handling in advance.

Derive a **complete call-budget table**, including model qualification,
development, diagnostics, natural prefixes, every main cell, controls, repeats,
reloads and bounded contingency. Do not copy an inherited development allowance
without expanding its stages. Separate physical attempts from logical policy
costs. Give conservative GPU-hour sensitivities with loading, downloads, idle
allocation, exports and interruption reserves. Distinguish the adopted budget
from proposed amendments, and leave real throughput explicitly unmeasured.

Set concrete capability, protocol, numerical, memory, resource and precision
gates. They must not select a favorable recovery effect, hide a failing model or
turn scientific failures into retry opportunities. Explain what a failed gate
would require us to reconsider.
