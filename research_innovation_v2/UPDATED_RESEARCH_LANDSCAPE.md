# Updated research landscape

Search and inspection date: **4 October 2026**. The prior investigation ended September 30. This update includes manuscripts first posted September 30/October 1 and a September-27 revision that the earlier notes missed. It is a broad, targeted, adversarial search, **not an exhaustive systematic review or proof of absence**. Primary methods, assumptions and experimental scope drive the conclusions; search dates are not manuscript dates.

Source details, inspected sections, versions, queries, code-access limitations and source discrepancies are in [recent agent literature](sources/RECENT_AGENT_LITERATURE.md), [its search ledger](sources/search_log_recent.md), [transition novelty](sources/TRANSITION_RECEIPT_NOVELTY.md), [cross-field sources](sources/CROSS_FIELD_SOURCES.md), and [root search ledger](sources/ROOT_SEARCH_LEDGER.md). External authors' reported results have not been reproduced here.

## Status map of the actual open question

**Established:** before/after events; correct-summary delta updates; dependency invalidation; current versus historical authority; correction/preservation metrics; source-only reset; context cleanup; completion reconsideration; same-state intervention.

**Crowded:** new recovery graph, version wrapper, generic typed transition, candidate-only context, simple recovery router, obligation ledger, or retained-state salvage claim.

**Assumption-dependent:** minimal safe selective recovery with opaque calls; global guarantees from local checks; historical noninterference while retaining evidence selected by earlier reasoning; inferring sufficient search from current receipts.

**Experimentally untested in this repository:** every LLM recovery advantage, natural prefix information gap, exposure saturation, receipt effect, and actual hardware throughput. CPU constructions establish possibility and algebra only.

**Plausibly worth testing:** whether the value of retaining a *real collaborative history* changes when the correction interface explicitly supplies the former input, and whether that difference occurs immediately before another peer round can reacquire the missing information. The inspected primary work does not establish that particular interaction on this observation model. This is a bounded empirical opportunity, not a first-ever guarantee.

**Useful even when no new arm is warranted:** an exact recovery-information atlas that separates missing historical operands, discarded alternatives, distributed ownership, current-evidence availability, and bounded computation. Such analysis prevents misleading interpretations of the existing B/C/L/F study.

## Why the completion competitor does not win by default

The earlier branch correctly identified that valid positive artifacts need not justify stopping. Its best mechanism can be implemented by known control/membership invalidation, and newer obligation/commitment systems add novelty pressure. Meanwhile, its finite-query adapter, completion enforcement and new primary estimand replace more of the PS. No observed efficacy or feasibility advantage offsets that cost. See the [independent competitor review](reviews/COMPLETION_COMPETITOR.md).

## Remaining literature uncertainty

The OpenReview *Repair Sets Before Repair Choices* route and PACMI full-method uncertainty remain unresolved from the earlier record; they are not converted into invented evidence. Some primary metadata are inconsistent (notably ESC-CR's displayed history versus identifier); the ledger preserves that discrepancy and does not rely on a normalized date. ICR's code landing page did not expose source files to the reader. TRACE's repository tree was inspected, but its entire runtime was not reproduced. These limits prevent exhaustive novelty claims; they do not prevent stating a narrowly designed empirical question.
