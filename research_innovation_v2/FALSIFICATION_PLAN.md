# Attempt to kill the recommendation

Review date: 2026-10-04. Target: the [historical-transition × retention addition](RECOMMENDED_EXTENSION.md), not a predetermined claim that it works. Separate **admission gates**, **interpretation failures**, and **empirical rejection**. None authorizes dropping unfavorable worlds or changing the main analysis after outcomes.

## Strongest opposing explanation

The proposal may be an old CDC event wrapped in a clearer prompt. Small LLMs may react to extra numbers or stronger visual emphasis without exploiting useful prior computation. Current evidence or old outputs may already determine the former value; the receipt may add nothing. Even when it adds information, another communication round may recover it cheaply, so a first-round difference may not matter over the full horizon. Existing StateAuditor/wording research may already explain everything beyond the original PS.

The experiment earns a stronger contribution only by establishing an interpretable **interaction with retained history**, its observation conditions and its downstream consequences. “More explicit corrections sometimes help” is insufficient as a headline.

## Strong baselines and controls

| Rival explanation | Required comparison/evidence | What defeats the stronger claim |
|---|---|---|
| Fresh recomputation is adequate | Original C and F remain in the core | C recovers as well with comparable cost; no useful reuse/latency distinction |
| Ordinary retained history already carries the needed information | Original B; exact prompt/state audit | Before-value directly present or provably reconstructible, with no credible bounded-computation question |
| Receipt improves every policy similarly | BΔ−B and CΔ−C beside eta1 | Eta1 is precisely small while both gains are similar: interface benefit, not retention-specific principle |
| Tokens or emphasis explain the result | Same layout/instructions/caps, token accounting and frozen matching convention | Same-information presentation reproduces the benefit; absent such a control, keep interface-level language |
| Early copying only looks like recovery | All four rounds, final/stable correctness, stale values and U damage | Early improvement is followed by larger regressions or unacceptable collateral damage |
| A simple compact result is enough | Candidate-only retention is the strongest deferred content baseline | If later matched testing shows it captures the entire benefit, use the simpler state and downgrade richer-history claims |
| An exact host updater does the work | Inspect all agent inputs; deterministic update is evaluator-only | Any delta, gold coordinate, corrected candidate or relevance label enters the runtime |
| Receipt restores missing current evidence | Compare per-peer active source tables byte-for-byte | New branches receive additional unedited current records or cross-peer private candidates |
| Known recovery already implements it | StateAuditor/CDC/REVISE given the same records | The format/mechanism reduces exactly; drop algorithm novelty even if the empirical interaction remains useful |

Candidate-only and extra wording controls are not included in the minimal inference budget. This is an explicit limitation, not permission to claim those explanations have been ruled out. A later follow-up must be separately designed and budgeted; it cannot be silently appended after an attractive main result.

## Pre-inference falsification

1. **Visible-state audit:** freeze exactly what the normal correction contains. If old values are already explicitly present in both B and C, the planned factor is empty. Do not hide values from the baseline simply to create it.
2. **Historical-role semantics:** confirm the before-value is accurate as a previous input and excluded from current-source lookup/forwarding. A receipt must not resurrect v1 as current evidence.
3. **Proof audit:** indistinguishable worlds must match the claimed observable state, not merely one scalar. The executed full-B scripted witnesses meet a designated-peer boundary; they do not prove natural transcript frequency, tokenizer compliance, or four-round impossibility.
4. **Redundancy attack:** recompute the difference-vector-plus-anchor counterexample, maximum redundancy, complete-versus-local sorting distinction, and BFS alternative-path example. If the planned interpretation ignores any of these, reject it before inference.
5. **No-oracle audit:** edits remain generated before model outcomes; receipts use only actual archived input versions. No correct-candidate selection, D/U routing, oracle dependencies or task-specific repaired outputs.
6. **Budget gate:** if the full extension displaces an affordable 48-world core, breaks the pilot cap, deletes sham controls, or threatens paired completion, omit the live extension. The CPU atlas remains valid.

## During the bounded pilot

Use the PS's capability and protocol thresholds, not a positive eta1 criterion. The two added development pairs check that the common receipt schema fits the context and produces valid calls within cost. Do not tune historical labels or selected worlds until receipts win. If a systematic rendering defect is discovered, fix it uniformly and repeat only the justified checks within the same budget.

If the model cannot solve the core tasks, stop the study under its existing rules; do not interpret failure as a transition-information limit. If first-round exactness is universally at a floor, eta1 may be uninformative even when later AUC varies. Report this admission concern before main execution; it cannot justify quietly switching the focal outcome after inspecting main data.

Natural history may contain no nontrivial missing historical operand. This rejects the **information-deficit explanation**, not necessarily every presentation or computational-help hypothesis. Do not engineer main transcripts, choose only successful prefixes, or use a semantic judge to manufacture the desired stratum. If the remaining question is merely prompt wording, demote the extension's thesis role.

## After execution: explicit outcome decisions

- **Large, sustained beneficial interaction with clear observation conditions:** retain as the added empirical contribution. Report direct effects, uncertainty, failed slots and scope. Avoid universality or algorithm priority.
- **First-round benefit only:** report earlier correct responses before new peer messages. Communication/recomputation savings need corroborating trace/representation evidence; salience remains an alternative. No measured inference savings; all scheduled calls were still made.
- **Opposite sign:** inspect direct effects. A receipt may aid reset, hurt retention or both; none is an automatic failure of the experiment. Reject any prewritten story about preferential incremental reuse if unsupported.
- **Harmful historical-value use:** do not recommend universal receipt disclosure; preserve the negative boundary and clarify temporal-role failures without claiming hidden mechanisms.
- **Precise near-zero interaction:** a bounded eta1 establishes stability of the immediate retention contrast only. Stability of the changed-event AUC reset contrast additionally needs a bounded eta_A. Equal large receipt benefits can produce zero interaction, so omitting receipts needs bounded direct benefits/harms and cost evidence as well. Simpler correction is not justified by a null interaction alone.
- **Wide interval:** inconclusive. Do not call equivalence, claim no value, or pool peers/rounds to create artificial power.
- **Only oracle-stratified correct summaries improve:** report that conditional result as offline analysis; a deployed policy cannot assume it knows those summaries are correct.
- **Same-information wording accounts for the result:** downgrade to an interface/presentation finding. Such a result may be useful but is not the intended information-contract contribution.

Predeclare eta1 as the focused secondary and eta_A as descriptive trajectory support. Selecting whichever looks stronger afterward collapses the causal-design claim. When all paired outcomes coincide, retain bounded-mean uncertainty rather than treating a zero bootstrap width as certainty.

## Novelty-collapse conditions

The algorithm claim already collapses to established before-image/transition systems and must not be made. The narrower empirical novelty claim would collapse if a primary predecessor implements the same retention × receipt contrast under equivalent observations and evaluates the same immediate/distributed recovery question. In that event, present the new evidence as replication or generalization. Different task names alone do not restore originality.

If the only defensible result is a small, noisy wording effect, this addition has not made the project substantially more original. Keep it in limitations or a diagnostic appendix and let the original exact recovery study carry the thesis. Do not escalate to a semantic extractor, extra reviewer or learned optimizer to protect the story.

## Kill the supporting exposure claim as well

Observed delivered-content paths form an envelope only for the modeled channels. Missing expected messages, recipient choice, source acquisition and other control/selection signals may carry additional influence. A graph ignoring these cannot certify full causal independence. A large call cone and small D are different units; no ratio proves unnecessary invalidation. If cone reset equals C, do not spend calls on a renamed duplicate. If natural traces add no insight beyond the protocol's construction, omit the metric from the headline.

## Evidence favoring the unchanged PS

Prefer the original study with its existing analysis if the receipt factor is empty; no affordable decisive comparison exists; all new mechanisms are redundant with B/C or known baselines; a clearer prompt fully explains the effect; or the original study already delivers the important quality/reuse tradeoff with better precision. Completion reopening is not the automatic fallback: it must independently pass its natural-prefix, generic-reconsideration, matched-observation and scope gates.

The purpose of these rules is to permit a clean negative conclusion. A disciplined decision that no additional runtime mechanism is warranted is preferable to an unsupported new-method claim.
