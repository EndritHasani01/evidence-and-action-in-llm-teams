## Major threats and unknowns

Before-images and transition-aware repair are established in databases and StateAuditor. CIR/ICR crowd generic causal metrics, routers and content ablations. Receipts change information and presentation together; padding does not eliminate attention effects. Claim the runtime-interface interaction, not hidden arithmetic reasoning.

Natural prefixes may reveal/reconstruct old values, lack useful summaries, or collect all current sources. First-round exactness can floor. Only 16/12 focal clusters means small effects may be inconclusive. Early improvement can regress; no scheduled calls are saved. A near-zero eta1 establishes only immediate-contrast stability, not a small eta_A or negligible direct receipt benefits.
